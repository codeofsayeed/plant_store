Our Story 🌿

Born from a love of plants and a dream to bring nature into every home. Meet the passionate people behind Blooming Oasis.

Where It All Began

In 2018, Amara and Daniel — two university friends with an obsession for houseplants — decided to turn their shared balcony jungle into something bigger. What started with a folding table at the local weekend market quickly grew into a beloved destination for plant enthusiasts across Melbourne.

Their mission was simple: make the beauty of nature accessible to everyone, regardless of how much space, time, or experience they had. They wanted people to feel the same joy, calm, and wonder that a single thriving plant can bring into a room.

Today, Blooming Oasis ships hundreds of plants each week and serves a community of over 12,000 happy plant parents. But the heart remains the same — a deep, genuine love for plants and the people who grow them.

Our Sourcing & Values

Every plant in our store is grown responsibly. We're committed to eco-friendly practices at every step of the journey.

🌍
Ethical Nurseries

We partner exclusively with nurseries that prioritise sustainable growing practices and fair worker conditions — locally and internationally.

♻️
100% Recycled Packaging

All our packaging is made from recycled or biodegradable materials. Zero single-use plastics — ever. Even our tape is paper-based.

🌱
Responsible Cultivation

We avoid invasive species and only stock plants ethically propagated — never taken from the wild. Biodiversity matters to us.

🌳
5% to Reforestation

Five percent of every sale goes directly to urban reforestation projects in partnership with Trees for Life. Over 5,000 trees planted and counting.

Meet the Plant Enthusiasts 🌿

The passionate team behind Blooming Oasis. Each one a dedicated plant parent first.

  • Amara Johnson

    Co-Founder & Head Botanist

    With a degree in Horticulture from Melbourne Uni and 10 years of hands-on growing experience, Amara curates every plant in the Blooming Oasis range. She is obsessed with Aroids and has over 200 plants in her own home.

  • Daniel Torres

    Co-Founder & Creative Director

    Daniel combines his background in graphic design with a passion for biophilic spaces. He's responsible for the visual identity of Blooming Oasis and manages all styled plant photography and interior plant styling content.

  • Mei-Ling Park

    Plant Specialist & Care Coach

    Mei-Ling runs our live chat support and leads our community care workshops. She has an extraordinary talent for diagnosing sick plants and has helped thousands of customers save their struggling leafy companions.

  • Oliver Chen

    Logistics & Sustainability Lead

    Oliver ensures every plant arrives healthy and every box leaves zero environmental guilt. He oversees our eco-packaging initiatives and coordinates our Trees for Life donations with meticulous care.

  • Jasmine Osei

    Community Manager

    Jasmine manages our vibrant social media community of plant lovers. She produces the plant care tip videos, behind-the-scenes content, and hosts our monthly virtual plant swap events that have become hugely popular.

  • Ryan Nakamura

    Nursery Operations Manager

    Ryan oversees all nursery relationships and ensures our supply of healthy, ethically grown plants. He visits partner nurseries personally every quarter and has an encyclopedic knowledge of tropical species.
