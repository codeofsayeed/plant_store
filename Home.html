About Blooming Oasis

Founded in 2018 by two passionate plant enthusiasts, Blooming Oasis started as a small balcony garden and grew into a beloved plant destination. We believe every home deserves a touch of green — and every plant deserves a loving home.

We source our plants from eco-conscious nurseries, use 100% recyclable packaging, and donate 5% of every sale to urban reforestation projects.
